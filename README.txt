Originally built for the Shelly Wall Display, this Homey-powered app turns any screen into a touch-optimized smart home dashboard. It runs a local server directly on your Homey and delivers real-time device control in any browser: wall displays, tablets, phones, or desktop. Enjoy real-time device control, sensor readings, a live energy flow diagram, one-tap flow triggers, camera snapshots, lock management, and PIN-protected alarm control.

Devices are organized by room or shown as a flat list. Tap a room title to collapse or expand its tiles. Tap to toggle, slide to dim, or long-press to drag tiles into any order. Select Homey flows to appear as buttons on the dashboard and trigger them with a single tap — with an optional confirmation dialog.

Lock tiles show current state and open a modal with all available actions (Lock, Unlock, and any extra modes your lock supports). Alarm tiles open a modal with Armed, Partially Armed, and Disarmed options — all PIN-protected. Energy price tiles highlight the current price green, yellow, or red based on the cheapest and most expensive hours in the next 8 hours.

Dark and light mode, fully customizable design settings (tile width, tile height, font size, accent color, title, tile shape, background style, animations), and a built-in energy dashboard make it a complete smart home interface for any screen. Device tiles show values, status and name aligned to the bottom for a clean, consistent look.

Tap any speaker tile to open the media player — album art, track info, volume slider, and transport controls. After a configurable idle delay the album art fills the screen with a Ken Burns zoom effect; tap the cover directly to jump to fullscreen immediately.

Display profiles let you configure what each individual display shows. A default profile applies to all displays. Add IP-specific profiles to restrict individual displays to selected devices and flows — ideal for multi-room or multi-display setups.

Add the dashboard to your home screen on Android or iOS for a full-screen standalone app experience without the browser address bar (PWA support).
