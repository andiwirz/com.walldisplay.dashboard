Turn your Shelly Wall Display into a smart home control panel powered by Homey. The app runs a local server directly on your Homey and delivers a touch-optimized dashboard with real-time device control, sensor readings, a live energy flow diagram, one-tap flow triggers, camera snapshots, and PIN-protected alarm management.

Devices are organized by room or shown as a flat list. Tap a room title to collapse or expand its tiles. Tap to toggle, slide to dim, or long-press to drag tiles into any order. Select Homey flows to appear as buttons on the dashboard and trigger them with a single tap — with an optional confirmation dialog.

Dark and light mode, fully customizable design settings (tile width, tile height, font size, accent color, title, tile shape), and a built-in energy dashboard make it a complete wall-mounted smart home interface. Device tiles show values, status and name aligned to the bottom for a clean, consistent look.

Tap any speaker tile to open the media player — album art, track info, volume slider, and transport controls. After a configurable idle delay the album art fills the screen with a Ken Burns zoom effect; tap the cover directly to jump to fullscreen immediately.

Display profiles let you configure what each individual Shelly Wall Display shows. A default profile applies to all displays. Add IP-specific profiles to restrict individual displays to selected devices and flows — ideal for multi-room or multi-display setups.
